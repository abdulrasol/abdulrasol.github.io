Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Subul Technology (“SubulTech”, “we”, “us”) handles information in SubulTech Identity and our applications, including Rafuf (رفوف). By using these services, you acknowledge this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, Google account subject identifier, and profile image URL when you choose Google Sign-In.
  • Optional profile information: country and city when you provide them.
  • Application information: application, subscription, plan, redeem/promo activity, and session status.
  • Rafuf workbook references: Google Spreadsheet ID and display name that you select or create for Rafuf. We do not copy the spreadsheet contents into SubulTech Identity.
  • Operational information: request identifiers, security/audit events, device binding information for supported Baytraq staff sessions, and technical logs needed to operate and protect the service.

Google data and why we request access

Google Sign-In is used to identify you, create or connect your SubulTech account, issue an application session, and restore your application data on another device. Rafuf requests Google Drive file access only so you can choose, read, create, and update the Sheets files used as your ledger. The drive.file permission is limited to files created by or explicitly selected for the application.

We do not request or store your Google password. We do not store Google access tokens or the OAuth client secret. We store the stable Google subject identifier, basic profile fields, and the avatar URL. Google API data is used only to provide the user-requested application features and is not sold, used for advertising, or used to determine creditworthiness.

How we use information

  • Authenticate you and prevent account abuse.
  • Connect you to the correct application and subscription.
  • Restore your selected Rafuf workbook on another device after you sign in again.
  • Provide support, security, auditing, and service improvements.
  • Produce aggregate statistics such as users by country/city. Reports are not intended to expose another user's private workbook data.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your Google password, payment card details through Google Sign-In, the full contents of your Google Drive, or spreadsheet data in the SubulTech Identity database. Rafuf reads and writes the selected Sheet directly through Google APIs according to the permission you grant.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure providers required to operate the service, including Cloudflare Workers/D1, Cloudflare Pages, Cloudflare Access, Google OAuth/Drive/Sheets APIs, and error or hosting services configured for a particular application. These providers process data under their own terms and applicable agreements.

Retention and deletion

We retain account and subscription information while your account or an active business record requires it. You may request access, correction, export, or deletion by emailing privacy@subultech.top. We may retain limited records where necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, or resolving disputes. To remove Rafuf's Google permission, you can also remove SubulTech/Rafuf from your Google Account's third-party connections.

Security

We use HTTPS, hashed passwords and tokens, scoped access controls, Cloudflare protections, and audit logging. No online service can guarantee absolute security; please protect your Google account and device.

Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the services change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made. For privacy questions or requests, contact privacy@subultech.top.
