Key Features

Key Features

Core Operations

🛒

Fast point of sale

Multiple carts, search and camera or scanner barcodes, and direct cash / electronic pay buttons — hide either method if you don't use it.

🖥️

Desktop cashier: keyboard, mouse & touch

Two-pane screen: products and top sellers beside the cart table, shortcuts (F2 cash, F3 electronic, Space to type a quantity…) and large touch targets.

🧮

Rounding without editing prices

Cashiers can't change prices; they round the total down to 250, 500 or 1000 and the difference is recorded as an invoice discount.

🧾

Manual invoicing

Full invoices with discounts, part payments and PDF printing — no POS screen required.

📦

Inventory & stock-take

A movement card per item, physical counts in cartons + pieces with variances, weighted-average cost and low-stock alerts.

Inventory & Purchasing

📦

Sell and buy by carton or piece

Define packaging per item (carton, pack…) with its own price and barcode, then sell or receive by packaging or by piece; stock and cost stay in pieces.

🏷️

Retail & wholesale prices

Optional wholesale price per piece or packaging, and a retail/wholesale switch per invoice that reprices the cart instantly.

⏳

Expiry tracking

Track expiry lots where needed and get alerted to items nearing expiry.

🚚

Purchase intake

Manual entry, scanning or CSV import with drafts, item matching, cost, expiry and amount paid.

↩️

Documented returns

Invoice-linked returns (by piece or by carton) with allowed quantities, reason and settlement, reflected in stock and customer balance.

Customers, Money & Team

👥

Customers, suppliers & debts

Accounts, payments, balances, partial and credit invoices in one easy-to-trace ledger.

💸

Expenses

One-off and recurring expenses with due alerts and their effect on results.

⏱️

Shifts & cashier

Sales separated from collections and cash, with clear drawer reconciliation at shift close.

🔐

Roles & permissions

Staff roles, approvals for discounts and sensitive actions, PIN or QR sign-in.

Insights & Printing

📊

Reports & insights

Sales, margins, expenses, debts, period comparison and top products.

🏷️

Barcodes & printing

Barcode labels per piece and per carton, receipts, PDF invoices and thermal printers.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it right for a one-person business?

Yes — selling, invoices, stock, customer accounts and expenses all work without a team.

Do I need internet all the time?

Core operations run locally. Internet is only needed for cloud sync, activation and update checks.

Where is my data stored?

On your device by default. It goes to the cloud only if you enable the Cloud plan. See the Privacy Policy.

Does it charge bank cards?

Electronic payment entries only record how a sale was paid; there is no payment-gateway or bank-terminal integration.

Is it a certified accounting or tax system?

No. It provides operational accounts and reports and does not claim tax certification or government e-invoicing compliance.

Can it read a purchase invoice photo automatically?

That feature is coming and currently disabled. Available now: manual entry, scanning and CSV import.

Which platforms does it run on?

Android, iOS, Windows, macOS and Linux. Some features differ by platform (e.g. LAN linking in browsers).

How many languages?

16 registered languages including Arabic and English; translation completeness varies by language.
