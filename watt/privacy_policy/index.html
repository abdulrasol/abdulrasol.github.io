Privacy Policy

Watt

Last updated: 2026-10-23

1. Introduction

This policy explains what Watt (formerly Taqati) collects, why, and the choices you have. Watt is published by AbdulRasol Al-Hilo (Subul Technology). By using the app you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

Account. You sign in with Google or Apple. We receive your name, email address and profile picture from the provider. We never see your Google or Apple password.

Profile. Phone number, country and city, account type (user, technician or company) and, optionally, a picture.

Your calculations. Appliances, loads, settings and the systems you save or add manually.

Location. Only if you allow it. Your coordinates, or a place you pick on the map, are used to get sun and weather data for your site.

Company and store content. Company name, description, address, logo, products, prices, images, quotation requests, offers, carts and orders, including the delivery details you enter at checkout.

Feedback and support. Your message, optional phone number and optional image.

Device and usage. A notification token, app version, platform (Android, iOS or web), language, and anonymous usage statistics from Firebase Analytics.

On your device. Settings, session state and cached climate data are stored locally so the app works faster and offline.

3. How we use it

  • Run your account and keep you signed in
  • Calculate and save your systems and fetch climate data for your site
  • Operate the store, quotations and orders between buyers and sellers
  • Send notifications you have not switched off
  • Answer feedback and support requests
  • Keep the app secure, prevent abuse and improve reliability
  • Meet legal obligations

4. Services that process your data

Watt relies on the following services. They receive only what they need to work.

  • Firebase (Google): sign-in, database, notifications, remote settings and analytics.
  • Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple: to verify your identity.
  • PVGIS (European Commission, Joint Research Centre): receives the coordinates and system settings you request, to return solar data. Requests may pass through a Cloudflare proxy operated by us to make them more reliable. The proxy keeps a temporary cache of the climate response and does not link it to your account.
  • NASA POWER: receives coordinates to return climate data when PVGIS is unavailable.
  • Open-Meteo: receives coordinates to return weather and the solar outlook.
  • OpenStreetMap services (map tiles and place search): receive the area you view and the place names you search.
  • Our image and file storage: stores pictures you upload, such as product images and logos.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

  • Sellers and buyers: when you place an order or request a quotation, the other party sees the details needed to complete it, such as your name, phone number and delivery address.
  • Public content: a company profile, its products and its offers are visible to other users.
  • Service providers listed in section 4.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, and to investigate fraud, abuse or security incidents.

6. Permissions

  • Location: to fetch solar data for where you are. Optional.
  • Camera: to take pictures and scan codes. Optional.
  • Photos: to choose pictures to upload. Optional.
  • Notifications: to send order, offer and update alerts. Optional.
  • Network: required for online features.

You can change permissions in your device settings. Without a permission, the related feature may not work.

7. Account deletion and retention

You can delete your account permanently from your account page in the app. You confirm with Google or Apple again, and your cloud and local data are removed. You can also follow the steps on the account deletion page, or write to us.

We keep data only as long as needed to provide the service. Some records, such as completed orders, may be kept for the period required to resolve disputes, prevent fraud or meet legal duties, and are then removed or anonymized.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including access rules on the database and encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

Watt is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will remove it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to some processing. Write to us and we will reply within a reasonable time.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant we will tell you in the app.

Analytics

We use GoatCounter to collect anonymous traffic statistics (such as page views and referral sources). GoatCounter does not use cookies and does not collect any personally identifiable information.
