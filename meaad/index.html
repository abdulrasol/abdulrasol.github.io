FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Start simply, then activate the features your workflow needs.

Can I use MEAAD without creating an account?

Yes. Free Local lets you manage customers, services, and payments on your device without signing in.

Where is my local data stored?

Your local records are stored on your device. Cloud features are separate and require an activated account.

What is the difference between Free Local and Cloud?

Free Local covers everyday work on one device. Cloud adds synchronisation and higher limits when your account is activated.

Is the Cloud plan a recurring subscription?

No. Activation is handled as a one-time licence request. Contact support from the app for availability and details.
